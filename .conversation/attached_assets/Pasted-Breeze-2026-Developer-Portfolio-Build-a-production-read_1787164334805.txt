This is NOT a generic developer portfolio, resume website, SaaS landing page, or template.
The goal is to create a portfolio that feels like a premium interactive digital product launched in 2026 — the kind of website someone could realistically expect from an elite engineer/designer working at a company such as Apple, Microsoft, Anthropic, Vercel, or another world-class technology company.
The website should immediately communicate:
This person builds serious technology — and knows how to present it.
The visual quality, motion design, typography, interaction design, responsiveness, performance, and attention to detail matter as much as the content.
